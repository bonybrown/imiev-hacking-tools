# 0x110

| Field          | Value      |
|----------------|------------|
| **Sender**     | ETACS      |
| **Recipients** | EV-ECU     |
| **Period**     | On demand  |
| **Length**     | 1          |

Likely immobiliser seed request from ETACS to the EV-ECU. In the observed handshake, this frame precedes the EV-ECU challenge frame [0x100](100.md) by about 2 ms.

---

## Signals

| Byte | Signal   | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| 0    | Command? | Observed as `0x00` in available captures |

## Example

Observed request:

```text
110#00
```

This is followed by the EV-ECU challenge in [0x100](100.md).
