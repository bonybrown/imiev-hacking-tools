# 424 (0x424)

| Field          | Value       |
|----------------|-------------|
| **Sender**     | ETACS       |
| **Recipients** | EV-ECU, BMU |
| **Period**     | 40 ms       |
| **DLC**        | 8           |

Car lights, locks and timer status from the ETACS unit.

---

## Signals

| Byte | Bits | Signal               | Notes                                                                           |
|------|------|----------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 0    | 4    | Rear fog lights      | `set_not_clear_08` in EV-ECU                                                    |
| 0    | 3    | Front fog lights     | `set_not_clear_04` in EV-ECU                                                    |
| 0    | 0    | ?                    | `set_not_clear_00` in EV-ECU                                                    |
| 1    | 6    | Park lights          | `set_not_clear` in EV-ECU                                                       |
| 1    | 5    | Drive lights         | `set_not_clear_07` in EV-ECU                                                    |
| 1    | 4    | ?                    | `set_not_clear_09` in EV-ECU                                                    |
| 1    | 3    | Front wipers active  | `bVar2` in EV-ECU                                                               |
| 1    | 2    | High beam            | Not read by EV-ECU; identified by OBDZero only                                  |
| 2    | 7    | ?                    | `set_not_clear_06` in EV-ECU                                                    |
| 2    | 2    | ETACS/meter fault    | `set_not_clear_05` in EV-ECU; `etacs_or_meter_fault` in BMU. Triggers DTCs U1108 and U1109 |
| 4–5  | 15:0 | Minutes counter      | uint16, big-endian; 0xFFFF = invalid; read by both BMU and EV-ECU               |
| 6    | 3    | Rear defrost active  | Not read by EV-ECU; identified by OBDZero only                                  |
| 7    | 3    | ?                    | `set_not_clear_03` in EV-ECU                                                    |
| 7    | 2    | ?                    | `set_not_clear_02` in EV-ECU                                                    |
| 7    | 1    | ?                    | `set_not_clear_01` in EV-ECU                                                    |
| 7    | 0    | ?                    | `etacs_something_5_bits` in EV-ECU [^1]                                         |

[^1]: EV-ECU reads byte 7 as a 5-bit mask (`byte[7] & 0x1F`), then extracts bits [3:0] individually for DTC control. Bit 4 is included in the initial read but is never individually used.
